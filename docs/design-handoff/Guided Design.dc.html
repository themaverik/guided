03 — Annotation System
signature element — drawn on the canvas

Annotations are drawn, selected, dragged, and snapped directly on the page canvas. The tools + palette ride in a floating toolbar over the canvas, and the inspector opens as a popover anchored to the selection — the side panel is never the drawing surface (too small); it only lists shapes + numeric properties. The detail panels below document the toolbar/inspector contents.

05 — Connectors & Flow
FigJam-grade · square + curved · snap to objects

Every shape (rectangle, square, polygon) and every connector elbow carries a corner radius (default 8px) — no hard 90° corners. Connectors route Straight · Curved · Square and their ends snap to object anchor points (edge mid-points, shown in blue).
